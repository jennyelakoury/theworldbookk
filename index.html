Questions

What age is it for?

Any age. Little ones colour the flags, older kids read the stories, and grown-ups usually learn something new too.

What do I get?

One 397-page A4 PDF: a welcome guide, a colouring page and an English and Arabic fact page for each of the 195 countries, and a certificate page at the end.

Is it in Arabic?

Yes. Every country has a fact page in both English and Arabic, so children can learn in either language, or both.

What does “2 books” mean?

You get two copies for $6.99 instead of $9.98: one for your family and one to give to a friend, a cousin or a classroom.

Which countries are included?

All 193 member states of the United Nations, plus Palestine and Vatican City, in alphabetical order.

Can we colour it on an iPad?

Yes. Open the PDF in a drawing or note app such as GoodNotes or Notability, or use the Markup tool in the Files app, and colour with an Apple Pencil or a finger. No printer needed, and no paper either.

Do I need a colour printer?

The small reference flags look best printed in colour. You can also print in black and white and look at the reference flags on a tablet or phone.
